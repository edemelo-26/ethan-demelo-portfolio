Vending Machine Tracker

Where are beverage vending machines in Texas A&M’s East Quad, and how reliably are they stocked? Our group mapped their locations and recorded the drinks and stock in the sample.

My contribution
I collected georeferenced vending-machine points and attributes with ArcGIS Field Maps and produced maps as part of the group project.
One finding
Our team found that Langford Architecture Building A had the widest variety and highest drink stock in the collected sample.
