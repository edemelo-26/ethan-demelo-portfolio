What this demonstrates

The submission documents four object tables—People, Classes, Classrooms and Buildings—and three relationship tables connecting people/classes, buildings/classrooms and classes/classrooms. The relationship diagram makes those connections inspectable.

I also created and ran a ClassListQuery. This was a course exercise in relational structure and querying, not a production student-information database.
